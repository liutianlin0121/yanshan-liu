# 才元刘氏先世著作

**网址：<https://tianlinliu.com/yanshan-liu/>**

直隶盐山县才元刘氏世居盐山县才元里，在今河北黄骅市旧城镇。这个网站整理才元刘氏先人的著作和生平资料，从清初十世泽霖公起，到清末民初为止，有原书影印、标点原文和白话，可以逐句对照着读。

## 网站有什么

- **著作**：先人成部的书，每部都配原书影印，原文与白话逐句对照，带注释和校记。
- **人物**：十八篇先人小传，配世系图。每篇的资料单独列出（方志、家谱、档案、碑传的原文、白话和影印），另有考订。资料可以单篇下载，也可以合集下载。
- **文章**：没有成书的单篇诗文二十六则，散见于方志、诗钞、朱卷和家谱，每篇单独一页。
- **搜索**：全站检索原文和白话。
- **整理花絮**：录文是怎样一轮轮校出来的，以及先人宦游、科举、姻亲的图表。
- **待查书目**：已知、但还没有找到原书的先人著作。

## English

**Writings of the Liu Family of Caiyuan** — <https://tianlinliu.com/yanshan-liu/>

The Liu family of Caiyuan lived for generations in Caiyuan, Yanshan County, Zhili (today Jiucheng Town, Huanghua, Hebei). This site collects the writings and biographical records of the family's ancestors, from Liu Zelin (10th generation, early Qing) to the early Republic. Each text is presented with scans of the original edition, a punctuated transcription, and a modern Chinese translation, aligned sentence by sentence.

- **Books**: eight works, including *Lifeng jianyan* (Liu Zelin's records from his prefectship of Fengxiang), poetry collections, a commentary on the *Spring and Autumn Annals*, and biographies, with notes and collation records.
- **People**: eighteen biographies with a family tree. Each is built from local gazetteers, the family genealogy, Qing archives, and epitaphs, with the sources shown alongside and available for download.
- **Articles**: twenty-six shorter pieces (poems, prefaces, examination essays) scattered across gazetteers, anthologies, and the genealogy.
- **Search** across all original texts and translations, a **making-of** page on how the transcriptions were checked, and a list of ancestral works not yet located.

The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages; pages are generated by scripts in a separate project and should not be edited by hand here. Transcription, punctuation, translation, and notes were prepared by Tianlin, of the 22nd generation, with the help of Claude. Corrections are welcome through the feedback link on each page; descendants of the Caiyuan Liu family are especially welcome to get in touch.
